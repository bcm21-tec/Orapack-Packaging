Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What is your typical turnaround time?

Our standard turnaround time is 5-10 business days depending on the order complexity. Rush orders can often be accommodated for an additional fee. Please contact our sales team for specific timeline requirements.

Do you offer customization options?

Yes! We specialize in custom packaging solutions. You can customize materials, size, shape, printing, and design. Our design team will work closely with you to create the perfect packaging for your needs.

What is your minimum order quantity?

Minimum order quantities vary depending on the type of packaging and customization level. We can handle orders from small batches to large volume production runs. Contact our sales team to discuss your specific needs.

Do you offer eco-friendly options?

Absolutely! We offer a wide range of sustainable packaging solutions including recycled cardboard, biodegradable materials, and compostable options. These are environmentally responsible choices while maintaining quality standards.

Do you provide design services?

Yes! Our expert design team can help you create custom packaging designs from scratch. We provide free consultations and can create 3D mockups for your approval before production.

Can you handle international orders?

Yes, we ship internationally to most countries. Shipping costs and delivery times vary depending on the destination. Contact us for a detailed quote including international shipping costs.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, bank transfers, and PayPal. For large orders, we also offer customized payment terms. Contact our accounting department for more information.
